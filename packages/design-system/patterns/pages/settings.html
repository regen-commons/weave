What is Certified?
What it is
A free account for your profile and your work that does not belong to any one app. The Hypercerts Foundation runs it.
Why Weave uses it
So you do not need a separate Weave account. The same sign-in works in other apps that use Certified.
How you sign in
With your email address. Certified sends you a code, so there is no password. If you are new, you can join there and come straight back.
What Weave learns
Who you are on Certified, and nothing else. Weave does not keep an email address from sign-in, and you do not need a public project there.

Install Weave on your phone

Add Weave to your home screen and it opens like an app, full screen.

iPhone or iPad
Open Weave in Safari and tap Share. If you do not see it, tap the three dots first. Then choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add.
Android
Open Weave in Chrome and tap the three-dot menu. Then choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

On an iPhone or iPad the installed app starts empty, because it does not share what Safari keeps on the device. Save your initiative first, then sign in from the app to open it there.
