# Tech & Sun (TAS) Hubs: Documentation & Blueprint

An open-source, replicable blueprint for solar-powered, internet-connected community hubs.
Built by Greenpill Nigeria · Live pilot at NAU/UNIZIK, Awka · techandsun.com

## What is a TAS Hub?

A **Tech & Sun (TAS) Hub** is a solar-powered shipping-container facility that provides a community, most often a university campus, with three things **steady electricity, steady internet, and a safe physical space to learn and build.**

Each hub combines:

- **Off-grid solar power** (panels, charge controllers, inverter, lithium battery bank)
- **Satellite internet** (Starlink)
- **Optional Ethereum staking infrastructure** (a separately approved revenue-compute path)
- **A furnished co-working / co-learning interior** (desks, chairs, storage, workshop space)
- **Smart metering and impact-tracking integrations** (Switch Electric, GreenGoods, Karma GAP)

The result is infrastructure that keeps running when the grid, the ISP, or the fuel supply doesn't — and a physical anchor for community programming: workshops, ReFi/Web3 education, and local builder support.

This repository is the open documentation framework for that model: architecture, a planning BOM, construction sequence, operations, and evidence standards. It helps a community begin a disciplined local design; it does not let anyone safely replicate a hub without site-specific engineering, procurement, permits, and commissioning.

## The Problem

Nigeria — sits on a specific, painful contradiction: a large, technically capable, crypto- and tech-aware youth population, with almost none of the reliable physical infrastructure needed to turn that awareness into building.

Three gaps compound each other:

1. **Unreliable electricity.** Chronic grid instability forces reliance on petrol generators, which are expensive, loud, polluting, and themselves unreliable at the fuel-supply level.
2. **Weak, inconsistent internet.** Even where power exists, connectivity is often too slow or unstable for real technical work — remote collaboration, node operation, structured online learning.
3. **No stable shared physical space.** Students and builders lack a secure, always-on location equipped for deep work, experimentation, and community learning — as distinct from a lecture hall or a cybercafé.

The result: talent exists, awareness exists, but neither compounds into capability because there is nowhere reliable to do the work.
