# Sources of the extraction eval's files

Every document here is invented for the eval, except two excerpts of real projects,
included with their owner's consent and under their own licences:

- `green-goods-readme.md` and `green-goods-readme.pdf`: the opening of the Green Goods
  README, from [greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods)
  at commit [`e600fce`](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/blob/e600fceed823336d20b248f9c1e25db9b1609b72/README.md),
  Copyright (c) 2024-2026 Greenpill Dev Guild, under the MIT License. The licence badge's
  link was made absolute; nothing else was changed.
- `tech-and-sun-overview.md` and `tech-and-sun-overview.pdf`: based on Tech & Sun (TAS)
  Hubs Documentation by Greenpill Nigeria, licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Original:
  [Greenpill9ja/tas-documentation](https://github.com/Greenpill9ja/tas-documentation),
  at commit [`6d17c4a`](https://github.com/Greenpill9ja/tas-documentation/tree/6d17c4a1c355283815881081958015233def3481).
  Credit: Tech & Sun Hubs / Greenpill Nigeria. Changes: these are excerpts, "What is a
  TAS Hub?" from the README and "The Problem" from `docs/01-overview/README.md`; the page
  header was rewritten from HTML to text, its "V1.0 reference" sentence and the logo and
  badges left out. The excerpts are shared under the same licence.

The PDFs and the `.docx` are made from the Markdown in this folder and in `sources/` by
`make-files.mjs`, so they can be made again; see the extraction section of
[the evaluation README](../README.md#add-files).
