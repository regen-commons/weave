# App dialect

Weave runs on phones first, often on weak connections, for people who may visit once a month. Plain task screens carry the work.

- **Theme:** a pinned theme is drawn from the first paint, by a file of the site's own and with no change to the security policy (DL-097). Otherwise follow the system. Settings has System, Light and Dark, a wide screen's header has a switch between light and dark (DL-079), outlined to match Sign in beside it (DL-107), and a phone's You tab has the full choice as chips (DL-086).
- **Type:** base 16 px. Inputs stay at 16 px, so phones do not zoom. Reading text uses `reading-lg` in conversation and `reading-md` in cards.
- **Navigation:** the product name top-left returns home. Home, Partners, Funding and Initiatives are separate pages. Bottom navigation on phones, fixed to the bottom of the window and clear of the home indicator; header navigation from 900 px. A phone's app bar holds the save status and Sign in, and its You tab holds the theme and the way to Settings (DL-086). Focused steps keep the name and save status, and on a phone dock their way forward where the tab bar would be (DL-089); every page has the footer, spanning the window as the app bar does (DL-102), without its list of Weave's pages on a phone where the tab bar shows (DL-093). A page inside a section shows the way back in the app bar on a phone, and breadcrumbs above its title from 900 px (DL-080). The steps of describing an initiative show the step bar alone (DL-085). A page that is reloaded, or that a phone dropped in the background, opens where it was left, and the installed app's icon offers Funding, Initiatives and Your Initiatives on a long press (DL-092).
- **Touch:** every control answers a press at once, with no flash from the browser and no hover look left behind after a tap. The browser's bar and an installed app's status bar take the page's surface in either theme (DL-087). A field tells the keyboard what it holds: a name starts each word with a capital, a links field gets the address keys with corrections off, and Enter is labelled Search or Done only where that is what it does (DL-088). A sheet pulls away from its content as well as its grip, and stands on the on-screen keyboard (DL-091). A page deeper in is pushed on and the way back up pops it off (DL-094). A tab is pressed, not held like a link (DL-095). The page never moves or bounces sideways (DL-098). In a form of several fields Enter on the phone's keyboard is Next and moves to the next field (DL-096).
- **Lists:** full-width card grids, equal heights and two-line title space. Search, filter menus, removable chips, count and sort share one bar, in the same place on every list. A list narrows as you type; only Home's search has a button (DL-081). On a phone Home's previews are rows that swipe sideways and rest on a card, and a list card leaves out a picture it does not have and draws its tags as labels (DL-090). Sort opens the same menu a filter opens (DL-100). A long list fills in as a person scrolls near its end, waits after three batches so the footer can be reached, and brings nothing unasked while the keyboard is in it (DL-101). A funding card counts the days to a deadline within 60, and tones it in the last 14 (DL-103). A loading list's skeleton holds the loaded page's exact room (DL-104). Pages align with the logo; narrow tasks stay at that edge.
- **Describing:** one page a step, with the step map above it and the record beside it on wide screens (DL-110). Talk It Out comes first: the button becomes Stop in place, the words appear in a box whose rows are always there, and the fields under it fill in place. Typing in the same box does the same. Say where it runs: on this device. Where the browser has no speech on the device, say so in one line. Nothing moves when it is pressed: results and problems go in a Status slot, and choices that need room open in a dialog (DL-113).
- **Pencil and ink:** every derived value starts in pencil. Keep, Change and Remove are explicit decisions; Remove offers Undo. Confirmed corrections survive a rebuild. Unfinished edits block continuing and survive reload.
- **Saving:** `rc-save-status` is always visible during the intake. Offer saving inline once per seam, never as a modal that arrives unasked. A dialog closes on a press outside or Escape (DL-082). Sign in is one press that goes to Certified and comes back to the same page, and a question that replaces or withdraws something is asked in a dialog (DL-085). See [08-accounts-and-saving.md](../../../docs/platform/08-accounts-and-saving.md).
- **Texture:** the mycelium lies behind every page, under its content, and cards and fields cover it (DL-078). It is fixed to the window and drawn as the website draws it, with trails that fade where a mouse or a finger moves over a light resting network (DL-106). A form or a side panel with no card of its own sits on the page itself, where the resting network is at its quieter strength (DL-099). No grain on task screens.
- **Offline:** the three steps work offline with a typed draft. A notice says when something is waiting for a connection.
- **Performance:** first paint under two seconds on a mid-range phone. Models load only on a clear opt-in, with size and a cancel button.
- **Device draft:** a browser keeps several, and each says "Not saved yet" until it is saved. Say plainly when this browser could not store the latest changes. Two tabs never stop each other: the latest edit wins and the replaced version is a device backup (DL-112). Keep full source words and file bytes; the short public card is editable. An unread file or page must say so. Links are references until a fetch adapter exists.
- **Needs and offers:** two Ask lists, side by side on wide screens and stacked on phones, each led by Talk It Out and one box. Show the active count out of four, every applicable kind, and say when more were heard than fit (DL-111). Kept cards stay fixed when words are read again; removals offer Undo. A card's details change in a dialog.
- **Finish:** show the checked initiative, needs and offers together with first funding and the Share card. Private initiatives see counts only. Unknown live counts stay unknown; invented matches require the development fixture label. Saving and sharing actions arrive with their Step 7 adapter.
- **Funding:** use the whole public catalog for visitors, with no fit before a checked initiative exists. Keep source dates, unknowns, readiness and research-priority groups separate from thematic fit. Exact program links never substitute another call. Optional applicant details stay local and outside intake. Team slots show private counts or shared candidates only in labelled development examples until requirements and services are approved.
