Money is one of eight kinds of support

An initiative also runs on skills, relationships, land and culture. Every need and offer in Weave names its kind, an idea from the eight forms of capital.

  • Land, water, seeds, soil and living systems.
  • Tools, materials, buildings, equipment and vehicles.
  • Research, data, methods and recorded knowledge.
  • Practical skills learned by doing and shared by teaching.
  • Trust, introductions, partners and people who can help.
  • Grants, loans, donations and payment for the work.
  • Art, stories, music, language and shared cultural practices.
  • Care, ritual, meaning and connection to place.
