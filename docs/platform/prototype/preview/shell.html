About this prototype

This is the Weave app itself with its built-in examples, from 10 November 2026. Names and records are fictional. Nothing you type leaves this page, and Restart clears it.

What changed

  • Describe is one screen with one Continue. Talking it through is still there when you want it.
  • Needs and offers are one page. Type one, add it, and tap a kind of support to add or remove it.
  • Signing in with Certified only says who you are. Saving is its own tap and says where your work goes.
  • A Certified project is linked or created as its own step. Linking reuses its details, and creating reuses yours.
  • Sharing for matching and publishing on Certified are separate, each with a review of what leaves.
  • Partners come as direct fits and possible fits, and start with a Certified project.

Still an example

Certified sign-in, projects, team workspaces, sharing and publishing are examples here, and nothing is sent. Projects, team workspaces, sharing and publishing still wait for their services.
