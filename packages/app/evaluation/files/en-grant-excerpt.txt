Project title: Coastal Mangrove Nursery
Organisation: Mangrove Futures Trust
Location: Lamu, Kenya

Summary
We grow mangrove seedlings in a community nursery and replant them with fishing families along the Lamu archipelago.

Budget request
We are requesting funding for nursery shade nets, a boat for planting trips, and wages for two nursery keepers.

What we bring
We will share our planting maps and train other villages in mangrove nursery care.
